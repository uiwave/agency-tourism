# Machu Picchu

Enjoy a unique trip to Machu Picchu with personalized service in small groups. You will travel by train through the Sacred Valley, explore the incredible sanctuary of Machu Picchu with a guided tour and return comfortably to Cusco. An unforgettable experience at the most important archaeological center in South America.

## Short Itinerary

### Day 1

- 04:00 am: Pickup from your hotel in Cusco
- 06:10 am: Tourist train from Ollantaytambo to Aguas Calientes
- 08:00 am: Bus to Machu Picchu (30 min)
- 08:30 am: Entrance and guided tour (2 hours)
- 10:30 am: Free time to explore on your own
- 12:30 pm: Return to Aguas Calientes
- 01:00 pm: Lunch (not included) and free time
- 02:30 pm: Train back to Ollantaytambo
- 04:30 pm: Van transport to Cusco
- 08:00 pm: Approximate arrival in Cusco
